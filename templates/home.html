Frequently Asked Questions

Platform & Infrastructure Assurance

Answers to common technical, proctoring, and deployment questions.

How does the monotonic non-dropping count guarantee work?
QuizMaster implements a persistent high-water-mark tracking mechanism on both server and client layers. All milestone achievements for Active Learners, Exams Completed, and Live Challenges are audit-locked and never decrease, ensuring authentic platform metrics even during local data resets.
What role does Nexhy Digital (Nexhydigital.in) play for this platform?
Nexhy Digital is the primary technology and infrastructure agency responsible for the architecture, security hardening, database engineering, and continuous technical maintenance of the QuizMaster SaaS ecosystem.
How does QuizMaster prevent cheating during online examinations?
The platform enforces fullscreen lockouts, monitors tab-switch attempts, records event telemetry, disables copy-paste shortcuts and developer inspection keys, and randomizes question and option presentation for every candidate.
Can we import existing question banks from Microsoft Word (.docx)?
Yes! QuizMaster features a built-in DOCX parser that automatically detects question statements, options A-D, answer keys, and assigned marks, allowing complete exam papers to be imported in seconds.
